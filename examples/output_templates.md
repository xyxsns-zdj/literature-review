# Output Templates — All Deliverables by Step

This file contains templates for every output required by the 6-step workflow. Each template maps to a specific step and completion gate.

---

## Step 2 Output: Topic Statement

```
Topic: [Broad area]
Focus: [Narrowed focus within broad area]
Scope: [Date range], [study types included]
Purpose/Significance: [Why this review is needed; how it differs from existing reviews]

Example:
Topic: Oncolytic viruses and cancer immunotherapy
Focus: Molecular mechanisms (ICD/cGAS-STING/TME), genetic engineering, combination therapy, clinical translation
Scope: 2015–2025, preclinical and clinical studies, DNA and RNA virus platforms
Purpose: To provide an integrated four-domain synthesis that bridges mechanistic principles with translational evidence, 
addressing gaps in biomarker validation, systemic delivery, and OV-CAR-T clinical translation not covered by existing organ-specific or platform-specific reviews.
```
