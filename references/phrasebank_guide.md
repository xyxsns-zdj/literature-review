# Manchester Academic Phrasebank — Quick Reference for Literature Reviews

Based on the [University of Manchester Academic Phrasebank](https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk/). Used in Step 5 of the literature_review skill.

---

## 1. Introducing Your Review

**Establishing importance:**
- "X is a major area of interest within the field of..."
- "In recent years, there has been an increasing interest in..."
- "X has emerged as a powerful tool for..."

**Establishing a niche (identifying gaps):**
- "Despite this progress, several questions remain unanswered."
- "However, far too little attention has been paid to..."
- "The relationship between X and Y remains controversial."

**Presenting purpose and scope:**
- "This review argues that..."
- "This paper critically synthesizes recent advances in..."
- "The scope of this review is limited to..."

---

## 2. Referring to Sources (Synthesis)

**General descriptions of research:**
- "Research consistently demonstrates that..."
- "Several studies have established that..."
- "There is a growing body of literature that recognises..."

**Synthesizing multiple sources (use these instead of listing authors):**
- "A number of studies have found that... (reference 1; reference 2; reference 3)"
- "The evidence for X is compelling (reference 4; reference 5)"
- "Research on X has yielded mixed results. While some studies report... (reference 6; reference 7), others demonstrate... (reference 8; reference 9)"

**Expressing your stance:**
- "This interpretation is supported by..."
- "However, these findings should be interpreted with caution because..."
- "A more compelling explanation is that..."

---

## 3. Being Critical

**Identifying weaknesses:**
- "A major limitation of this study is..."
- "The research to date has tended to focus on X rather than Y."
- "These findings are limited by the small sample size / short follow-up / single-center design."

**Comparing and contrasting:**
- "In contrast to earlier findings, more recent evidence suggests..."
- "While Smith (2020) found X, Jones (2021) reported Y, potentially reflecting..."
- "These contradictory results may be explained by differences in... between the two studies."

**Identifying gaps:**
- "What is not yet clear is the impact of X on..."
- "No previous study has investigated..."
- "Questions have been raised about the validity of..."

---

## 4. Writing Conclusions

**Summarizing:**
- "This review has identified several key findings..."
- "Taken together, these studies support the notion that..."
- "The evidence reviewed here suggests that..."

**Implications:**
- "These findings have significant implications for the understanding of..."
- "The present review raises important questions about..."
- "This synthesis provides a framework for..."

**Future directions:**
- "Further research should be undertaken to investigate..."
- "Prospective validation of these biomarkers in randomized trials is urgently needed."
- "Future studies should address the current limitations by..."

**Concluding statements:**
- "The translational trajectory of X underscores..."
- "As our understanding of X deepens, Y is poised to become..."
- "Addressing these gaps will require concerted effort on..."

---

## 5. Transitions Between Sections

**Moving to a new topic:**
- "Having discussed X, this section now turns to Y."
- "Beyond the mechanistic considerations, the clinical translation of..."
- "These insights have directly informed the engineering of..."

**Linking back to an earlier point:**
- "As discussed in Section 2, the STING pathway..."
- "Building on the TME reprogramming principles outlined above..."
- "This observation underscores the delivery challenge discussed earlier."

**Previewing what's next:**
- "This TME reprogramming creates a permissive context for the combination strategies discussed in Section 4."
- "The remainder of this section addresses three key aspects of..."

---

## 6. Common Mistakes to Avoid

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use Instead |
|---------|---------------|
| "Smith (2020) found... Jones (2019) reported..." | "Regarding [theme], research indicates... (Smith 2020; Jones 2019)" |
| "This paper will review..." | "This review argues that..." (must have a thesis) |
| "Many studies have been done on X" | "Over 200 studies published between 2015 and 2025 have examined X, with consistent findings regarding..." |
| "More research is needed" (vague) | "Prospective validation of STING/ISG biomarkers in randomized Phase III trials with pre-specified biomarker-stratified analyses is the most urgent priority." |
| "It is interesting to note that..." | State the finding directly: "Notably, STING-deficient tumors showed paradoxically increased OV susceptibility." |
